Pyloxa Refund Policy

This policy is part of the Pyloxa Pro Terms. Pyloxa Pro is sold through Polar Software, Inc. (“Polar”), the merchant of record, so Polar issues refunds to you.

Effective December 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. 30-day refund on your first purchase

You can get a full refund within 30 days of your first purchase of Pyloxa Pro. You don’t need to give a reason. This doesn’t apply to renewal charges (see section 6).

2. Full refund if a provider disallows subscription use

Pyloxa runs the official command-line tools you install and sign in to yourself. If Anthropic or OpenAI clearly does not allow its tool to be used through your subscription with Pyloxa, for example by saying so in its terms or official policy, and we announce on pyloxa.com or in the app that we are disabling that subscription connection, you can get a full refund of your most recent payment. Ask within 60 days of our announcement. This applies even after the 30 days in section 1, and also when your most recent payment was a renewal.

3. How to ask

Use the customer portal linked from “Manage subscription” in the app or on your account page, reply to your Polar receipt, or write to support@pyloxa.com from the email address you bought with. Include your order number if you have it. We will not ask you to justify a request under section 1.

4. What happens to the money

Polar refunds the full amount to your original payment method, including the tax collected. How soon it appears depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. What happens to Pro

A refund ends the license for the period refunded.

  • If you refund your first purchase, Pyloxa returns to the free version the next time it refreshes its license: within about 24 hours if the computer is online, and no later than 14 days after the last successful refresh.
  • If a renewal is refunded under section 2 or the law, you keep the rights from the periods you paid for earlier: versions released before the refunded period began continue to work with Pro.

Work already running finishes. Your projects, data and settings are not deleted or changed.

6. Renewals, and after 30 days

Renewal charges can’t be refunded, and a first purchase can’t be refunded after 30 days, except under section 2 or where the law requires it. Your subscription stays active until the end of that year. You can cancel at any time; cancelling stops the next renewal, so cancel before the renewal date if you don’t want to renew.

7. Trial and Pro preview

The trial and the Pro preview are free and need no payment card, so there is nothing to refund.

8. Abuse and chargebacks

A refund may be refused in cases of suspected fraud or abuse, such as repeatedly buying and refunding. If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of asking for a refund, the license is revoked in the same way as for a refund.

9. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits a right to cancel or to a refund that the law of your country gives you and does not allow to be waived.
