Privacy Policy

This policy covers the pyloxa.com website, the Pyloxa desktop app and the Pyloxa account service at account.pyloxa.com. The free version of the app needs no account and never sends us your projects, code or prompts. It sends usage data only if you turn on anonymous usage statistics, which are off unless you turn them on. If you sign in for the trial, the Pro preview or Pro, the account is used only for licensing. The website counts visits, keeps your email address only if you join the Pro early-bird list, and publishes your name only if you ask to be listed as a founding supporter.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Pyloxa is made by ChiYu-Huang, an individual developer in Taiwan (“we”, “us”). We decide how the personal data described here is used. Write to support@pyloxa.com about anything in this policy. Email sent to that address is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to our Gmail inbox, where it is kept so that we can answer and follow up; ask us if you want your messages deleted.

The website

Hosting and fonts

pyloxa.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub receives your IP address and logs it for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages visitor. The site’s fonts are served from pyloxa.com itself, so no font service receives your IP address. Cloudflare answers the DNS lookup for the domain.

Visit statistics

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics runs on every page. It counts page views, referring sites, countries, browser types and page load speed. It uses no cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors.
  • Google Analytics 4 runs on the home pages and the page-not-found page, but only for visitors outside Europe (see below). It records page views, clicks on download links and the fact that the early-bird form was submitted (not the address you entered). Advertising features and Google signals are off. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Event-level data is kept for up to 14 months; we see only aggregated reports.
  • Visitors in Europe: before loading Google Analytics, the page reads your device’s time zone. If it is in Europe, which covers the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, or in one of their overseas regions, or if it is UTC or can’t be read, the page doesn’t load Google Analytics at all and Google receives nothing from it. Only Cloudflare Web Analytics counts your visit.
  • Cookies: elsewhere, Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_PWKKJHQV92, which last up to two years, to tell new and returning visitors apart. If Google still locates you in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, it sets no cookies. You can block these cookies in your browser or install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Tour video on the home pages

The home pages show a cover image for our tour video, served from pyloxa.com. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the page loads Google’s YouTube player from youtube-nocookie.com, and YouTube receives your IP address and browser details under Google’s privacy policy, as it would on youtube.com. If Google Analytics is running on the page, it also records that the video was started. The “Watch on YouTube” link opens the video on youtube.com.

Pro early-bird list

If you enter your email address in a “Notify me” form, the address goes directly to Buttondown, the service we use to send email. Buttondown sends you a confirmation email first, and you join the list only after you confirm.

  • What is kept: your email address, tags showing that you joined the Pro early-bird list and which language page you used, and when you signed up. Buttondown also records technical data such as your IP address, browser and approximate location, as described in its privacy policy.
  • What we use it for: one email when Pro launches, with the early-bird offer. We don’t add you to other lists or share your address with anyone else.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email has an unsubscribe link. After you unsubscribe, Buttondown keeps a record so you are not emailed again; write to us if you want the address removed completely.
  • Joining is optional. Without it you only miss the launch email; downloading and using the free version need no email address or account. The early-bird list is separate from Pro accounts: signing in doesn’t add you to it.

Founding supporters page

Early-bird buyers can choose to be thanked by name on a page on pyloxa.com. After your purchase we ask you once by email from support@pyloxa.com. You are listed only if you tell us you want to be; nobody is listed by default.

  • What is published: the name or nickname you give us and, if you want, one link, such as your website or GitHub profile. We don’t publish your email address, what you paid or when you bought.
  • What we use it for: only to thank you on that page. We check the Polar order to confirm that you bought at the early-bird price.
  • How to be removed: write to support@pyloxa.com from the email address you bought with, and we remove you from the page within 7 days. Being removed doesn’t affect your license.
  • Public copies: the page is public. Earlier versions of the site stay in its public GitHub repository, and search engines or web archives may keep copies that we can’t remove.

The Pyloxa app

The free version needs no account

The free version needs no account and never connects to our account service. Pyloxa sends us no crash reports, project files, prompts or credentials, and it sends no usage data unless you turn on anonymous usage statistics. The statistics shown in Settings are computed on your computer.

Pro accounts (trial, Pro preview and Pro)

The trial, the Pro preview and Pro need you to sign in. The account is used only for licensing: it isn’t linked to anonymous usage statistics, and we don’t use it for marketing. Signing in opens your web browser at account.pyloxa.com, our account service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores its data in a Cloudflare D1 database in the Asia-Pacific region.

  • Sign-in: with Google, GitHub or a six-digit code sent to your email. From Google we receive only your verified email address and Google account ID (scopes openid and email); from GitHub, your verified primary email address and GitHub user ID (read:user, user:email). We don’t keep Google’s or GitHub’s access tokens. Sign-in codes are sent through Resend.
  • What we store:
    • Your email address and the sign-in methods linked to it, for as long as the account exists.
    • Each computer you sign in on: a random device ID created by Pyloxa (not a hardware fingerprint), a name that defaults to the computer’s name and that you can change, the last time it refreshed, and the Pyloxa and Windows versions. Kept until you remove the computer or delete the account.
    • Your license, trial or Pro preview, and the Polar order, subscription and customer IDs and the email address used for the purchase. Kept for as long as the account exists.
    • Security records of sign-ins, device changes and licenses issued, with the IP address of the request, kept for 13 months. Rate limits use your IP address for up to two days.
    • Records we write when we help you or give you Pro: notes and tags on the account, and the reason for and dates of any Pro we give you. If you work with us as a creator, also your name, platform, profile and content links, the tracking link or discount code we gave you, and the downloads and subscriptions we attribute to it. Kept for as long as the account exists and included when you download your data.
    • Which service notices we emailed to your account and when, kept for 13 months.
  • Daily license refresh: once signed in, Pyloxa refreshes its license at startup and about once a day, sending only the device ID, a device credential and the Pyloxa and Windows versions to account.pyloxa.com. If it can’t connect, Pro keeps working for 14 days from the last refresh.
  • Payment: buying Pro opens a checkout page from Polar, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, tax, invoices and refunds under its own privacy policy. We send Polar your account’s email address and a random account ID so the purchase is linked to you. We never see your card details.
  • Email we send: sign-in codes, trial reminders and service notices about your account and license, such as the end of the Pro preview, planned maintenance or Pro we give you, all through Resend. Replies go to support@pyloxa.com. We don’t send marketing email to accounts.
  • Email from support@pyloxa.com: when you write to us, we reply from support@pyloxa.com, and after you buy Pro at the early-bird price we send you one thank-you email asking whether you want to be listed as a founding supporter. These emails are also sent through Resend.
  • Notices in the app: the daily license refresh can bring back short service notices from us, such as planned maintenance, which Pyloxa shows as a banner. Nothing extra is sent to get them, and which notices you dismissed is kept only on your computer.
  • Pro preview: until December 31, 2026, signing in gives you Pro for free, and the app shows the end date at least 30 days ahead. From December 1, 2026 you can also buy Pro during the preview. When the preview ends you keep Pro for 14 more days, until January 14, 2027; after that the app returns to the free version and keeps your data and settings.
  • The account page at account.pyloxa.com/account lists your computers and license, lets you rename or remove computers, download your data as JSON and delete the account. It uses two cookies that are needed for signing in: pyloxa_signin during sign-in and pyloxa_account, which keeps you signed in on the account page for 7 days. It loads no analytics or third-party content.
  • Deleting the account removes your sign-in methods, computers, licenses, account-page sessions, security records, our notes, tags and creator records, and the records of notices sent to you, and replaces your email address with a placeholder. Our own log of changes we made to the account keeps only what was done and when. A record that each device ID has had a trial is kept without personal data so that the trial can’t be repeated. Polar keeps orders and invoices as its own rules and tax law require.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

Pyloxa can send us one anonymous report a day so we can see how many people keep using it, where new users get stuck and which agents fail most often. It is off unless you turn it on. Pyloxa asks once, after the first task you accept, and you can turn it on or off at any time in Settings → Usage and feedback. That page also shows the exact reports before they are sent.

  • What a report contains: a random anonymous ID created when you turn sharing on, and the date. It also contains:
    • Pyloxa’s version, whether it came from GitHub or the Microsoft Store, Windows 10 or 11, and the interface language.
    • The edition (free, trial or Pro), the week you first used Pyloxa, and whether you finished the setup guide.
    • For each CLI, whether it is installed and signed in, its major and minor version, and whether it uses a subscription or an API key.
    • Counts for that day: conversations, brain replies, plans, tasks and their result category (for example finished, quota exhausted or rate limited), how results were accepted, fallbacks, cross-tests, brain reviews and background-run actions.
    • Feature settings: brain review, cross-testing, concise replies, worktree mode, what closing the window does, and sleep prevention.
  • What it never contains: prompts, instructions, code, file names, paths, project or repository names, CLI output, error messages, account names, email addresses or API keys. The anonymous ID is not linked to a Pro account.
  • Where it goes: to our receiver at stats.pyloxa.com, which runs on Cloudflare. We don’t store your IP address or browser details, only the two-letter country code that Cloudflare derives from your IP address. The app contains no third-party analytics code.
  • How long: reports are kept for 13 months. After that, only daily totals with no ID are kept. If Pyloxa can’t connect, it keeps unsent reports on your computer for up to 7 days.
  • Your controls:
    • Turning sharing off stops sending and deletes the ID and unsent reports from your computer.
    • Delete my data asks the receiver to delete every report with your ID, then turns sharing off.
    • Reset anonymous ID starts over with a new ID.

Network requests Pyloxa makes

  • Update check: at startup and every 12 hours, Pyloxa asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest Pyloxa release and the CLI compatibility catalog. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to GitHub; it also carries Pyloxa’s version number, but no project or usage data. Turn it off in Settings → Notifications and updates.
  • Pro sign-in and license refresh: only after you sign in. Signing in opens your browser at account.pyloxa.com; after that, Pyloxa refreshes its license about once a day as described above. The free version never connects.
  • Notifications (Pro): only if you set them up, Pyloxa sends the project name, the event (for example waiting for you, a gate that didn’t pass, a finished plan or exhausted quota) and the plan or task title from your computer directly to the Telegram bot, Discord webhook or HTTPS webhook you choose. Those services’ terms apply, and the message doesn’t pass through us. Bot tokens and webhook addresses are encrypted with Windows secure storage.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: only if you turn them on, Pyloxa sends finished daily reports to stats.pyloxa.com about once an hour while it is open (see above).
  • Installing or updating a CLI: only when you click and confirm, Pyloxa runs the official install command, which downloads from npm or from the CLI’s provider.
  • Git and GitHub: fetch, pull, push and pull requests happen only when you choose them, through your own git remotes and your own GitHub CLI sign-in.
  • Agent tools on your computer: while Pyloxa is open, it listens on random ports on 127.0.0.1 so the agents it starts can search and propose shared memories and, with Agent Teams (Pro), read the team’s tasks and send messages to each other. Other computers can’t connect, and nothing is sent over the internet by these tools. Messages between team members become part of the agents’ prompts, so they reach the providers of the agents you use.
  • No spell-check dictionary: spell-check is off, so Pyloxa doesn’t download a dictionary. Versions 0.1.0 to 0.1.8 downloaded an English spell-check dictionary from Google (redirector.gvt1.com) the first time they started, which this page didn’t list. That request revealed your IP address to Google and carried no other data. A dictionary that was already downloaded stays in %APPDATA%\Pyloxa\Dictionaries, and you can delete that folder.
  • Links in the app, such as the changelog, open in your web browser, where the website section above applies.

AI agents

Pyloxa runs Claude Code, Codex CLI and Gemini CLI on your computer. Those tools send your prompts, your code and the files they read to Anthropic, OpenAI and Google under your own account or API key, and those providers’ terms and privacy policies apply. We never receive this content. Pyloxa does not read, copy or send the CLIs’ sign-in credentials. For Gemini CLI, Pyloxa uses a separate settings folder in which Gemini CLI’s usage statistics are turned off. Memories you keep in Pyloxa’s shared memory are added to the prompts of every agent you use, so they reach each of those providers.

Data stored on your computer

  • %APPDATA%\Pyloxa holds the projects you opened, conversations, plans, task records and logs, shared memory, settings and usage statistics. If you turn on anonymous usage statistics, it also holds the anonymous ID and any reports not yet sent.
  • API keys you add are encrypted with Windows secure storage and kept in the same folder. Pyloxa passes a key only to the CLI process that runs your task.
  • If you sign in, the license, the device ID and the device credential are encrypted with Windows secure storage in the same folder. While you are signed in, the account’s email address is also kept in that folder so that Settings can show it. Signing out deletes the license, the credential and the email address from this computer.
  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pyloxa\worktrees holds the git worktrees where agents work.
  • Only if you choose to: Sync to CLIs writes a project’s rules and preferences into AGENTS.md and related files in that project, and the user-level option writes rules and preferences into a marked block in ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md, ~/.codex/AGENTS.md and ~/.gemini/GEMINI.md, after backing up the originals to %APPDATA%\Pyloxa\memory-backup. The CLIs send what’s in these files to their providers when you use them.
  • Only if you choose to (Pro): Skills sync copies skills from Pyloxa’s library into the CLIs’ skills folders, such as ~/.claude/skills and ~/.agents/skills. A skill it would overwrite or remove is first moved to %APPDATA%\Pyloxa\skills-backup.
  • Uninstalling Pyloxa keeps these folders so that you don’t lose your history. To remove everything, delete both folders after uninstalling, then run git worktree prune in your projects.

Diagnostics and bug reports

Settings → Usage and feedback → Copy diagnostics copies your Pyloxa, Windows and CLI versions to the clipboard; nothing is sent. Issues on GitHub are public and covered by GitHub’s privacy statement, so never paste API keys, tokens or code you can’t share publicly.

Legal bases

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Your consent for the early-bird list, given when you confirm your email address. You can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Your consent for the founding supporters page, given when you ask to be listed. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to remove you.
  • Your consent for the app’s anonymous usage statistics, given when you turn them on. You can withdraw it at any time by turning them off, and Delete my data removes reports already sent.
  • Our contract with you for the account, sign-in, license and payment records, which are needed to provide the trial, the Pro preview and Pro.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure and preventing one license from being shared beyond its limits, for the security records and rate limits.
  • Our legitimate interest in supporting you and managing Pro we give to creators, for account notes and tags, creator records and service notices.
  • Our legitimate interest in running a secure website and knowing how many people visit and download Pyloxa, for hosting logs and Cloudflare’s cookieless visit counts. The site doesn’t load Google Analytics when your device’s time zone is in Europe.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, stop using it or delete it. These rights come from Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act and, for visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, from the GDPR, which also lets you object to processing, receive your data in a portable format and complain to your local data protection authority.

Write to support@pyloxa.com. For the early-bird list, write from the address concerned so that we can find it. For a Pro account, the account page lets you download your data and delete the account yourself; you can also write from the account’s email address. For usage statistics, use Delete my data in the app, or send us the anonymous ID shown in Settings → Usage and feedback; without it we can’t tell which reports are yours. We reply within 15 days and complete a request within 30 days. The visit statistics we see are aggregated and not linked to your name or email address, so we usually can’t pick out one visitor’s records.
