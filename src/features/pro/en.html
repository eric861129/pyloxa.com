Pro takes over what you would otherwise do by hand: several projects at once, decisions within the limits you set, takeovers when quota runs out, and finished work sent to another agent for testing. The account is used only for licensing

The free version needs no account and never contacts the license service. Once you sign in for the Pro preview or Pro, the account is used only to issue your license.

  • The license refreshes about once a day and sends only a device ID and the app and Windows versions. Your projects, code and prompts are never sent to us.
  • Each license works on 2 computers. Signing in on a third asks you to remove one first, at most 3 removals every 30 days.
  • If the license can’t refresh, for example when you’re offline, Pro keeps working for 14 days after the last successful refresh, with a reminder from day 3.
  • Details are in the privacy policy.

Going back to free deletes nothing

When Pro ends, running work finishes, and your settings are kept and paused, ready for when you come back to Pro. The app lists everything affected once, and marks each paused setting “needs Pro, paused” where it lives.

  • Several projects: one project keeps working and the others wait.
  • Auto within limits: new plans use suggest, then ask; approved plans keep running.
  • Automatic fallback: back to confirming each takeover.
  • My workflows: all kept; projects use the global settings for now.
  • Scheduled plans: kept, but they don’t start on their own; you can start them by hand.
  • Usage dashboard: locked, but usage is still recorded.
  • Skills library: the CLIs still read your skills, but you can’t edit them. Dispatch packs you already imported stay in effect.
  • If you stop subscribing (as opposed to the preview ending), versions released while you subscribed keep Pro for good.

The free version stays free

Everything to do with safety and correctness is in the free version: Pyloxa never touches CLI credentials, every task gets its own worktree, write scopes, verification commands, phase gates, and “unverified is never marked as passed”. The split between free and Pro was set before the preview began, and features that are free now won’t move to Pro.

Questions

When does the Pro preview end?

On December 31, 2026 (Taiwan time), and you can sign in and join until then. Everyone in the preview gets 14 more days of Pro after that, until January 14, 2027, then goes back to the free version with their data and settings kept.

How much is Pro, and how does the early-bird price work?

Bought between December 1 and December 31, Pro is US$49 a year at the early-bird price, once per person, and it stays US$49 for as long as you stay subscribed. The early-bird price is for people in the Pro preview: sign in from the app by December 31 and you’re in. From January 1 to March 1, 2027, Pro is US$59 a year; after that it’s US$79 a year.

If I buy in December, do I lose part of the free preview?

Your paid year starts on the day you buy, so in December the start of it overlaps with the free preview. What the early bird gets you is US$49 at every renewal.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full within 30 days of buying, no reason needed. You can also get a full refund if Anthropic or OpenAI clearly disallows connecting through your subscription. See the refund policy.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet; Pyloxa runs on Windows 10 and 11. When the Mac version comes out, the same license works on it.

What happens to Pro features if I stop subscribing?

Versions released while you were subscribed keep their Pro features for good; versions released after that run as the free version. This doesn’t apply to the Pro preview.

How many computers can use one license?

Two. Signing in on a third asks you to remove one first, at most 3 removals every 30 days.

Does signing in mean Pyloxa collects my code?

No. The account is used only for licensing. The license refreshes about once a day and sends only a device ID and the app and Windows versions. Your code and prompts go only where each CLI already sends them.
